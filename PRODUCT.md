# Noyce IDE

## Register
brand

## Users & purpose
Aerospace, automotive, medical, and industrial firmware teams (and the VCs who diligence them) need one Code-OSS workbench that produces DO-178C / ISO 26262 / MISRA evidence without sending source to a public cloud.

## Brand personality
Measured. Hardware-close. Auditable.

## Anti-references
Generic AI-purple SaaS. Cosmic lo-fi stock. Chatbot landing pages. Inter-on-slate dashboards.

## Principles
- Black workbench, Instrument Serif display, one cool accent.
- Show the product and the silicon, not a metaphor for them.
- Public CTAs point at noyce-ide-dist. No SPMS433 in marketing copy. No em dashes.
